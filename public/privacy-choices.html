Your privacy choices

We never sell or share your personal information, and we don't show ads or use advertising trackers.

Analytics on this website

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics to count which pages people visit, and Microsoft Clarity to see where pages are hard to use. Both load only if you allow them. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they never load.

Turn on JavaScript to change this setting. Without it, analytics never load.

Inside GO

The GO app, where churches plan trips and travellers keep their details, uses no third-party analytics at all. Your trip information stays with your church and with us.

For everything else, read our privacy policy. Questions: support@mkdscpls.com.
