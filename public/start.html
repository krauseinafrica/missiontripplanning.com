Set up your trip on GO

You have the checklist and the budget. This is what it looks like to stop running the trip out of a spreadsheet and an inbox.

Create your trip Free for your first 5 trips. No card required to start.

What setup actually involves

1 Create the trip

Name, dates, destination, and what it costs per person. Two minutes. Everything after this hangs off that one record, so the dates you enter drive the deadlines your team sees.

2 Add your team

Invite by email, or open applications and let people apply. Each person gets their own page for forms, payments and their fundraising total, so you stop being the place information goes to get lost.

3 Share the giving link

Every participant gets a page supporters can give to directly. Donations land against the right person automatically, which is the part that usually eats a treasurer's evenings.

What the free plan covers

  • Up to 5 trips
  • Unlimited participants on those trips
  • Applications, forms and e-signatures
  • Offline giving: record cash and checks against each person
  • Task lists and deadline reminders for the whole team
There is no platform fee on donations at any tier, including the free one. If someone gives $100 to a participant, $100 goes to the trip. The only deduction is what the card processor charges, which we do not take a cut of.

When you would actually pay

One reason, and it is worth being plain about it: taking donations by card online requires the Starter plan at $9 a month. The free plan handles the trip itself completely, but supporters have to give by cash or check and someone records it.

Most churches start free, run a trip, and only move to Starter when chasing checks becomes the annoying part. There is no trial clock and nothing expires, so there is no rush to decide.

Is this worth it for one trip?

Honestly, maybe not. A team of six going somewhere for a week, with one organised leader, runs fine on a shared document. GO starts earning its place when you are tracking money for more than a handful of people, or when the person organising the trip is not the person who has to answer "how much has Sarah raised?"

Set it up, add a couple of people, and see whether it fits how your church already works.
